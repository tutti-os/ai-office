# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Office creators who need to quickly generate, import, edit, and revisit documents, spreadsheets, and slide decks. They are usually starting from a prompt, a file, a blank canvas, or a template, and need the interface to make that starting point obvious without slowing down the workflow.

## Product Purpose

AI Office provides task-focused creation surfaces for documents, sheets, and slides. The home experience should help users choose the right creation path, browse useful templates, and return to recent work with confidence.

## Brand Personality

Calm, capable, and precise. The product should feel like a focused workspace: familiar enough to trust, polished enough to feel modern, and restrained enough that content and actions stay primary.

## Anti-references

Avoid decorative SaaS hero patterns, overly expressive marketing layouts, heavy visual effects, weak tab hierarchy, and inactive states that compete with the active workflow. Avoid making templates, history, and creation controls feel like equal-weight content when they serve different user decisions.

## Design Principles

- Make the next action legible within two seconds.
- Use clear hierarchy before adding decoration.
- Keep navigation vocabulary consistent across document, sheet, and slide surfaces.
- Treat density as a product affordance: compact where users compare options, generous where they make a primary creation decision.
- Preserve accessibility through high-contrast text, visible focus, predictable controls, and reduced-motion-safe transitions.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Aim for WCAG AA contrast for text and controls. Interactive areas need visible hover, focus, active, and disabled states. Do not rely on color alone to communicate selected tabs or categories. Respect reduced motion preferences for any future animation.
